# rig — died of ambition

- **Repo:** rig-repo (v1 through v4)
- **Grade:** died of ambition.
- **Fossil position:** the cautionary vertebra — the first cathedral.

`rig` was the super-harness dream, and it dreamed four times. v1 through v4, each a
larger attempt to build the everything-machine: the harness that would provision,
orchestrate, remember, and improve, all at once, all under one roof.

It never shipped. Not because the ideas were bad — several of them reappear,
sharpened, in KM1 — but because the surface area outran the hands building it.
Every version added more than the last version had finished. Ambition compounded
faster than completion, and a project where the unfinished work grows faster than
the finished work does not converge. It dies.

rig is the ancestor we point to when someone wants to make KM bigger. It is proof
that *reaching further* and *shipping* are different verbs, and that the first one
can quietly strangle the second. The dream was not wrong. The scope was.

The lesson KM took from rig's grave: decide what you are, ship that, and refuse the
next feature until the current one is done and honest. KM is one script on purpose,
in direct memory of the four rigs that tried to be everything and became nothing
you could run.

Not a failure — an iteration that bought a very expensive piece of knowledge:
know what you are, and stop there.
