# THESIS — why now

For three years the "10x" was a promise: any day now, an agent would make one
person as productive as ten. It stayed a promise because building the agent was
itself a cathedral project — months of harness, plumbing, and glue before the
first useful turn.

This decade it is a **shipping product.** The proof is `pi` itself: a coding
harness small enough to hold in your head, that extends itself on command, treats
models as configuration, and turned "build your own agent" from a cathedral into
an afternoon. The hard part — the harness — got solved, and given away.

So what is left? One inch. **Compute.** A great harness pointed at nothing is a
steering wheel with no engine. The last unsolved problem for a solo operator is
not *how do I build the agent* — it is *how do I get a thinking model, that I
control, running on hardware I can afford, right now, without a day of setup.*

**That is the inch KM closes.** Any box, one pasted SSH string, roughly ninety
seconds from a rental receipt to a thinking endpoint wired into a real harness.

The 10x, made personal, is a product of three things the Master now holds at once:

> **pi's extensibility  ×  KM's provisioning  ×  the operator's context.**

Extensibility he did not have to build. Provisioning that fails in seconds instead
of burning rental hours. And a harness that wakes up already knowing who he is and
what he is trying to do. Multiply them and a man with a phone, a cheap VPS, and an
H100 he rents by the hour is not waiting for the decade to arrive.

We do not wait for the decade. We want to see it now.
